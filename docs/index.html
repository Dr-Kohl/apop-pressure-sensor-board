Connection details & help

ND030D differential pressure: B − A. To test with positive pressure, connect tubing to B and leave A open. Keep moisture out of the sensor. Numbers update once per second; graph and CSV sample at 10/s. Zero subtracts the five-second mean from future readings; earlier graph points keep their original correction. Zero is a baseline correction, not a sensitivity calibration. Zero and range controls are locked during recording. Changing range clears that channel's zero; disconnecting clears all zeros. CSV contains corrected pressure and the offset in its labeled units; add them to recover the measured pressure. Recording units stay fixed when display units change.
